How are lettuce seedlings grown?

28 March 2026

Lettuce growing starts with the right seedling. Crop quality is decided in those first growth stages. Whether you grow commercially or in a garden, the basics of a healthy lettuce seedling do not change. A professional process, step by step:

1. Seed choice and quality

The first and most important step is seed that fits your region and growing season.

  • Type: Choose curly (Lollo Rossa / Bionda), Yedikule (Cos/romaine) or iceberg according to market demand or taste.
  • Certified seed: Disease-free seed with a high germination rate is essential for professional production.

2. Growing media: peat and trays

Unlike traditional methods, professional seedling production uses trays, which are critical for root health.

  • Mix: Sterile peat with perlite and vermiculite is ideal. It drains for the roots while holding moisture.
  • Cell count: 300-cell trays are typically used for lettuce.

3. Sowing depth and germination

The most common sowing mistake is burying seed too deep.

  • Depth: Lettuce seed is very small. About two to three times the seed diameter (around 0.5 cm) is enough.
  • Light: Some lettuce varieties need light to germinate, so cover only with a very thin layer.
  • Temperature: Ideal germination temperature is 10–15 °C.

4. Irrigation and feeding

Once seedlings emerge, the first watering period ends and controlled irrigation begins.

  • Misting: Use fine spray nozzles so seedlings are not damaged.
  • Feeding: When the first true leaves appear, a low-dose balanced N–P–K liquid feed can start.

5. Light and hardening-off

Seedlings grown under cover (greenhouse or indoors) need to be prepared for outdoor conditions.

  • Light: Enough sunlight is essential so seedlings do not stretch and stay weak.
  • Hardening-off: About a week before transplanting we reduce watering and gradually introduce plants to outdoor conditions.

İklim Fide note: stretching

If seedlings are thin and too tall, temperature is too high or light is too low. A healthy lettuce seedling should be compact, with a thick stem and dark green leaves.
