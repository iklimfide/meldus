Kanaryole Curly Lettuce Seedlings

French type, finely serrated and bright green

General characteristics

Type:Curly (Gentilina / French)
Colour:Light, bright green
Leaf:Finely serrated, crisp and flavourful
Harvest:45–55 days after transplanting
Season:Spring, autumn and winter

Growing advantages

  • Heat tolerance: Late bolting, so it can stay in the field longer.
  • Disease resistance: Resistant to many races of lettuce downy mildew (Bremia).
  • Market value: Attractive variety that forms large, heavy heads.
  • Shelf life: Holds freshness well after harvest.

Growing tips

  • Spacing: 25–30 cm between plants, 30–35 cm between rows.
  • Soil: Prefers free-draining soils rich in organic matter that hold moisture.
  • Irrigation: Regular watering is critical for crisp leaves and to avoid bitterness.
  • Feeding: Nitrogen-forward feeding speeds leaf growth.
